# Nihongaku Manabu — Learn Japanese Through Music

**Turn any song into a Japanese lesson.** Nihongaku Manabu is an offline Android
music and video player that overlays karaoke‑style Japanese lyrics — with
**furigana** over the kanji, **per‑word meanings**, and a **full‑sentence English
translation** — so you can read, sing along, and pick up vocabulary from the music
you already love.

Everything runs **on your device**. No account, no sign‑in, works offline.

---

## Features

- **Japanese lyrics, fully annotated** — furigana above each kanji, colour‑coded
  per‑word meanings, and a whole‑line English translation, aligned to the music.
- **Styled Lyrics scrolling** — the active line smoothly rises and highlights
  with a left‑to‑right progress sweep as the song plays.
- **Tap to learn, hold to peek** — tap a line to jump to it; press and hold a word to
  see its meaning while playback pauses.
- **Manual‑scroll mode for untimed lyrics** — pasted lyrics with no timestamps show
  every line fully annotated so you can simply scroll and read along while the song plays.
- **Get lyrics your way** — download timed `.lrc` lyrics in‑app (via LRCLIB), attach your
  own `.lrc`/`.srt` file, or paste plain lyrics and adjust the timing yourself.
- **Plays your music and videos** — local audio and video, background playback, media
  controls in the notification, an aspect‑fit/stretch toggle for video.
- **Audio equalizer** with Bose‑inspired presets, plus an audio‑reactive **visualizer**.
- **Home‑screen lyrics widget** showing the current line with its reading and meaning.
- **Fast, tidy library** — instant search by title/artist/genre and an iOS‑style A–Z
  fast‑scroll rail with a pop‑out letter bubble.

## How the lyrics work

1. Add a song or a whole folder from the **Library** tab.
2. Open the track and either **Get lyrics** (downloads timed lyrics), **attach** an
   `.lrc`/`.srt` file, or **paste** plain lyrics via *Edit subtitle*.
3. Furigana, word meanings and the translation appear automatically. On the first run,
   give the on‑device dictionary a few moments to finish loading.

> Japanese readings and English translations are generated **entirely offline** on your
> device — nothing about your lyrics is sent to a server.

## Privacy

Nihongaku Manabu is offline‑first: your music, videos and lyrics stay on your device, and
there are no accounts or analytics. The only data that leaves your device is an optional
song lookup when you tap *Get lyrics*, and the data Google AdMob uses to show the in‑app
banner. The microphone permission powers the visualizer only and audio is never recorded
or transmitted.
